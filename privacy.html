Privacy

Smoking Beards Library is a private tool run by CSGD FILMS (Dubai) to browse the Smoking Beards brand media stored in Google Drive. It is used by CSGD FILMS and the brand's marketing agency.

What it accesses. When you sign in with Google, the site asks Google for access to your Google Drive so it can show the files in the Smoking Beards library folder that have been shared with you. Agency accounts get read-only access. It also reads your name and email address to show who is signed in.

Where data goes. Nowhere else. The site runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Google. There is no server of ours and no analytics. A copy of the file list is kept in your browser to make the next visit faster; signing out and clearing the site data removes it.

What it changes. Read-only accounts change nothing. The administrator can upload files, create folders, add tags (stored as properties on the Drive files) and manage who has access.

Removing access. You can remove the site's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
